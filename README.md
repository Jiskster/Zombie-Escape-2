# Zombie Escape 2

This is a work in progress gametype.

It's a SRB2 gametype inspired on a gamemode plugin from Counter Strike by the same name and the sequel to the original SRB2 Zombie Escape 1.


## Escape
***Like the gametype says!***
- Survivors wins by escaping at the goal.
- Zombies wins by infecting all the survivors or escaping at the goal.
- There are maps with areas to hold up by a period of time for survival and/or even special gimmicks.

## Plague
***There is no escape...***
- Survivors wins by surviving until the round ends.
    - Everyone starts with Red Ring.
- Zombies wins by infecting all survivors.
    - Even survivors that dies without being infected becomes a zombie.
- Fall damage is enabled, be careful of heights.
    - Zombies have 8x times the damage.
