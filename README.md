# Zombie Escape 2

This is a work in progress gametype.

It's a SRB2 gametype inspired on a gamemode plugin from Counter Strike by the same name and the sequel to the original SRB2 Zombie Escape 1.

The goal is for the survivors to escape the area from zombie controlled players at the big ring being the goal.


- Survivors wins by escaping at the goal or killing all the zombies at once, even one singular survivor counts for escaping.
- Zombies wins by infecting all the survivors or escaping at the goal, even one singular zombie also counts.
- There are maps with areas to hold up by a period of time for survival and/or even special gimmicks.
